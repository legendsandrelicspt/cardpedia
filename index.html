A clearer way to collect

Good trades start with good ground rules.

Know what happens before you agree to a sale or swap.

01

Agree before you pay

Confirm the exact item, condition, total cost and tracked shipping with the other collector.

02

Keep proof of postage

For swaps, both collectors should use tracked delivery and share proof before marking items received.

03

Ask for a fair review

If something goes wrong, record the details and give both sides a chance to explain.

ⓘ

Prototype notice: Cardpedia does not hold payments, contact sellers or currently handle cases. Mediation screens are demos only — no request is sent and no payment is protected.
